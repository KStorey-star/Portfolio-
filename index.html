Chapter One

About me

I'm Katrina Storey, a communications and marketing strategist based on Long Island.

I earned my Bachelor's in Communications from Farmingdale State College. My work centers on one idea: brands grow when strong design meets real community engagement. I help businesses find their voice and build a real audience around it.

I work across social media campaigns, social listening, web design, and content creation. Together these keep a brand present, consistent, and worth following.
