Polar Seltzer

Bring Home the Bubbles

A social campaign built on the fans Polar overlooks most: the New Englanders who love the brand, then move away and can't find it anywhere.

ConceptSocial StrategyCreator ProgramUGC Engine
The Insight

Polar already has superfans. They just moved away.

For over 130 years, Polar has been a New England obsession. Three ingredients, wild limited-edition mystery flavors, and bottles that resell online the moment they sell out. Its quiet growth engine is the students who discover it in Boston or Worcester, then move away for work and spend years hunting for it in cities that don't stock it.

"I moved to Austin and the first thing I googled was 'where to buy Polar Seltzer near me.' Still looking."

That longing is an opportunity. Instead of chasing new audiences, the campaign turns homesick fans into a community that maps where Polar is missing, showing the brand exactly where to grow next.

The Concept

Bring Home the Bubbles

A year-round campaign that treats every transplanted fan as a scout. They tag where they find Polar in the wild, petition for stores that don't carry it, and unlock local pop-ups when a city hits enough requests.

It turns Polar's biggest frustration, "I can't find it here," into something fans do together. Every post is both content and a clue showing the brand where demand is strongest.

The Hook

A live "Polar Map" where fans drop a pin every time they spot it outside New England, turning the hunt into a shared game.

The Ask

"Not in your city yet? Petition it." Requests are counted by zip code, and once a city hits a target, it earns a real pop-up or a push to local stores.

The Payoff

Fans feel heard. Polar gets a heat map of real demand. And every new market launches with a crowd already waiting.

Where It Runs

Channels, each with a job.

Lead

Instagram

Home of the Polar Map and fan sightings. Stories push people to drop pins; the main feed celebrates milestones and pop-up reveals.

Reach

TikTok

The homesick humor lives here. Duets and stitches let every city add its own punchline.

Rally

Reddit & Facebook Groups

Transplant and college-alumni groups are where homesick fans already gather. This is where petitions take off.

Convert

Email & SMS

Zip-code sign-ups turn the map into a real demand list, then tell fans the moment Polar reaches their city.

Creators & Content

Who tells the story.

This runs on real voices, not big-name influencers, people who already know the feeling of missing a hometown brand.

Micro creators

Transplanted New Englanders

Creators with 10k to 80k followers who moved to cities like Austin, Denver, and Nashville. Real homesickness, and audiences that trust them.

Campus voices

College & alumni accounts

Partnerships with Boston, Worcester, and greater New England schools where the Polar habit is born.

UGC engine

Everyday fans

The real fuel. Shared sightings, duets, and petition wins turn fans into the campaign's biggest content team, at no extra cost.

Why It Works

What it does for Polar.

Turns scarcity into strategy. The frustration of not finding Polar becomes the campaign's engine instead of a reason fans drift away.

Builds a real demand map. Every pin and petition is real data pointing Polar toward its next market.

De-risks expansion. New cities launch with fans already waiting instead of a cold shelf.

Costs little, scales fast. Fans and small creators make most of the content, which keeps costs low and trust high.

Protects the brand's soul. It grows Polar nationally without losing the quirky, community-first identity fans love it for.

Feeds the flavor drops. The same map and fan list give every future mystery-flavor release a running start.

Why be flat when you can sparkle?
