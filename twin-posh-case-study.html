Hey Sis

A four-week brand voice and social campaign for Twin Posh, a twin-sister fashion boutique built on bold, going-out-ready style at a price every sis can afford.

Brand VoiceSocial StrategyContent CalendarMulti-Channel
The Big Idea

Every sis deserves to feel posh.

"Hey Sis" makes the whole feed read like a text from your best friend. The same warm greeting opens every post, caption, and story. It makes the brand instantly recognizable and turns casual scrollers into a community that shops.

"Hey sis 👋 new drop just landed and it's giving exactly what you need for the weekend. Come get it before your girls do."
Concept

A repeating "Hey Sis" greeting that ties every post together.

Duration

Four weeks of steady posting across Instagram, TikTok, Reels, and Facebook.

Purpose

Make every post feel personal instead of salesy, so followers turn into shoppers.

Campaign Goals

What "Hey Sis" was built to deliver.

The goals the plan was built to hit, and how each one would be measured.

+35%
Traffic to site & store

Steady, tagged posts that send followers straight to shop. Measured by visits coming from social.

+20%
Scrollers into sales

Every drop and restock paired with an easy call to action and shoppable tags. Measured by sales that come from social.

3×
Engaged following

Reels and TikTok bringing new fans into the community, aiming for 15% follower growth and triple the engagement.

+5%
The Result

Engagement grew 5% during the campaign, driven by Reels, micro-influencer collabs, and Stories built around polls that pulled fans into the conversation.

Message Platform

One tagline. Three ways in.

Pillar 01

You Deserve This

Affirming, confidence-first messaging around new arrivals and self-treat moments.

Pillar 02

Let Me Put You On

Insider-tip framing for styling advice, restocks, and early access.

Pillar 03

We See You

Community-first reposts, reviews, and shoutouts that celebrate real customers.

Brand Voice

She talks like your big sister.

Say this
  • "Hey sis, this sold out last time in 2 days"
  • "Okay but this fits like a DREAM"
  • "Tag your sis who needs this in her closet"
Not this
  • "Shop our exclusive new collection now"
  • Corporate, generic hashtags with no personality
  • Overly formal or pushy discount language
