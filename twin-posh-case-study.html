A brand launch case study
Hey Sis

Twin Posh is a fashion boutique bringing main character energy to every occasion. Built on bold looks, going out glam, and affordable finds, our brand is all about helping every sis look good, feel confident, and turn heads without breaking the bank.

Brand VoiceSocial StrategyContent CalendarMulti-Channel
The Big Idea

Every sis deserves to feel posh.

"Hey Sis" makes the whole feed read like a text from your best friend. The same warm greeting opens every post, caption, and story. It makes the brand instantly recognizable and turns casual scrollers into a community that shops.

"Hey sis 👋 new drop just landed and it's giving exactly what you need for the weekend. Come get it before your girls do."
Concept

A repeating "Hey Sis" greeting that ties every post together.

Rollout

One limited drop each week, only limited amounts and sizes, with anticipation built through teasers before every launch.

Purpose

Make every post feel personal instead of salesy, so followers turn into shoppers who show up the second a drop goes live.

The Result

Engagement grew 5% during anticipated weekly launch drops, driven by Reels, micro-influencer collabs, and Stories built around polls that pulled fans into the conversation.

How We Measured It

Built to sell out.

Every drop was limited on purpose, only limited amounts and sizes, so the community would show up the moment it went live. These are the numbers that told us if the anticipation was working.

Sell-Through Rate (STR)

The percentage of stock sold compared to the total inventory provided for that specific weekly drop.

Time to Sell Out

The exact hours, minutes, or seconds it takes for the dropped inventory to completely run out.

Week-over-Week (WoW)

The percentage change in sales or engagement comparing one week's drop to the previous week's drop.

Brand Voice

She talks like your big sister.

Say this
  • "Hey sis, this sold out last time in 2 days"
  • "Okay but this fits like a DREAM"
  • "Tag your sis who needs this in her closet"
Not this
  • "Shop our exclusive new collection now"
  • Corporate, generic hashtags with no personality
  • Overly formal or pushy discount language
