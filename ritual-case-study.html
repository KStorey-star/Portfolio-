The Challenge

A wholesale role, a consumer-facing feed

Ritual grew by selling straight to shoppers online, then moved into stores. Its LinkedIn never caught up. The feed still talks to shoppers picking a bottle off the shelf, not to the buyers deciding whether to stock it in the first place. For a wholesale role, that gap is the whole problem.

  • Recent posts are mostly press-mention reshares, not the brand's own story about how its products are made and launched.
  • No presence around events like Natural Products Expo West. That's the exact venue where brands meet retail buyers and distributors.
  • Traceable sourcing, which is core to the brand, is treated as a consumer trust signal rather than a reason a buyer can trust and stock it safely.
  • Nothing speaks to what a buyer actually wants to know: will it sell, what's the profit, and why does it deserve shelf space.
The Strategic Move

Write for the buyer, not the customer

Every post answers the buyer's real question ("why should I give this shelf space?") instead of the shopper's ("why should I take this vitamin?"). Same facts, aimed at a different reader.

Consumer framing

"Traceable sourcing means you can trust what's in every bottle."

Buyer framing

"Full traceability lowers recall risk and gets partners the sourcing paperwork they need, fast."

Consumer framing

"CBS named us Best Overall. Come find us on shelf."

Buyer framing

"That outside stamp of approval makes the pitch easier and the shelf decision safer."

Channel Strategy

Why LinkedIn leads, and what the rest support

Buyers make their decisions on LinkedIn and in trade press, not on Instagram. So LinkedIn carries the main pitch, and the other channels support it and point attention back to where the selling happens.

LinkedIn
Primary · Conversion

The main pitch lives here: sales proof, safety, market data, and leadership voice.

Instagram
Support · Credibility

Behind-the-scenes sourcing and event presence a buyer checks after seeing the LinkedIn post.

Email / Trade List
Support · Direct

A short note to the buyer list around the launch and after the show, sending clicks to the wholesale page.

Trade Press
Amplify · Earned

Pitching the story to industry outlets so the brand's own posts get outside backup.

How I'd Measure It

Reporting the way the role is actually judged

A plan without a target is just busywork. Here's what success would look like against where the page is today, and what I'd track to prove it.

Baseline today
~0.04%
Engagement rate: ~24 reactions against a 57K-follower page.
30-day target
2–3×
How much more the buyer-focused posts earn versus the old press reshares.
Leading signal
Clicks
Clicks to the wholesale page. Real buyer interest, not just views.
  • Impressions and reach by post type. Do buyer-framed posts reach a different audience than the consumer-style feed?
  • Engagement rate vs. follower count. The ~24-reaction average on 57K followers is the bar each post has to beat, not raw likes.
  • Click-throughs to a wholesale contact page. The clearest sign the content is pulling in real buyer interest.
  • Follower growth by job title and industry. Is the change bringing in more retail and distributor followers, which is the real goal?
  • A monthly recommendation note. Turns the data into one or two clear changes for next month's plan.
